# GO_TO_MARKET.md — Thriving App Strategy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

## The Philosophy

Build for ourselves first. Make it great for Nick and Erin. Then share it with the world.

The Thriving App isn't just a task manager — it's a framework for organizing your life around what actually matters. Pillars, goals, tasks, One Thing Mode. The product IS the philosophy. The philosophy sells the product.

## Phase 1: Dogfood (Now → MVP Complete)

**Goal:** Nick and Erin use Thriving every single day to manage real life and work.

What "done" looks like:
- Pillars, goals, tasks, subtasks fully functional
- One Thing Mode working and useful
- Domino Tree view for dependency visualization
- Document/photo attachments on tasks
- Reliable enough that Nick trusts it over sticky notes

**Why this matters:** If we won't use it daily, nobody else will. Every feature gets pressure-tested by real use before anyone else sees it.

## Phase 2: Content & Audience (Start During Phase 1)

**Goal:** Build an audience that cares about the *idea* of thriving before the app is public.

Content angles:
- **Building in public** — Share the journey of building Thriving (the AI dev pipeline, the product decisions, the philosophy)
- **The Thriving framework** — Life Pillars, goal hierarchy, One Thing focus. This is a philosophy, not just an app feature
- **Interviews** — Talk to people about how they structure their lives, what "thriving" means to them
- **Personal story** — Nick running a behavioral health company, building software, managing properties, being present for family. Real life organized around pillars.

Channels (pick 1-2 to start, don't spread thin):
- Short-form video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
- Newsletter/blog
- Podcast (interview format)
- Twitter/X (building in public thread)

**Key insight:** The content IS the top of the funnel. People buy into the framework first, then want the tool.

## Phase 3: Controlled Beta (After 30+ Days of Daily Use)

**Goal:** Get 10-20 real users and watch how they use it.

How to recruit:
- Audience from Phase 2 content
- Personal network (people who've expressed interest)
- NOT a public launch — hand-picked, personally onboarded

What to learn:
- Does the Pillars/Goals/Tasks model make sense to strangers?
- Where do people get confused without Nick explaining it?
- What features do they ask for that we didn't think of?
- What do they ignore that we thought was important?

**No pricing yet.** This is learning, not selling.

## Phase 4: Public Launch

**Goal:** Open signups with a clear value prop and pricing.

Prerequisites before launch:
- Beta feedback incorporated
- Onboarding flow that works without hand-holding
- Stripe integration for payments
- Landing page that explains the philosophy + shows the product
- At least one content channel with consistent audience

Pricing model (TBD, but initial thinking):
- Free tier (limited pillars/goals) to get people in
- Paid tier for full access
- Keep it simple — one paid plan, not three

## What Production-Ready Actually Means

It's not about the tech stack. It's about:
- **Reliability** — The app does what it promises without crashing
- **Onboarding** — A stranger can sign up and figure it out alone
- **Edge cases** — Handles weird inputs, slow connections, expired sessions
- **Data safety** — Auth works, data is isolated per user, backups exist
- **Performance** — Loads fast, feels responsive

## Things NOT to Do Yet

- Don't build a marketing site before the product works
- Don't add social features before single-user is solid
- Don't think about scaling before 100 users
- Don't spend money on ads before organic content is working
- Don't build a mobile app before the web app is proven
